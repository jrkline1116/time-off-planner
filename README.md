# Time Off Planner

A fill-in calculator that projects PTO, PSL and floating holiday hours paycheck by paycheck on the Spooner Physical Therapy payroll calendar.

- Biweekly paychecks; 27 pay periods in 2026 (the Jan 1, 2027 check paid Dec 31), 26 in 2027
- Salaried PTO rate is converted to an annual amount and re-spread across each year's paychecks
- PSL: 1 hr per 30 worked, 80/yr accrual cap, 40/yr use limit, 40-hr rollover
- PTO carryover capped at one year's accrual; floating holiday hours added for weekend holidays (expire in 12 months)
- FMLA leave dates: no PTO earned on leave workdays (prorated for partial pay periods); optional switch to stop PSL too
- One-page black-and-white printout

Nothing entered is saved. Single static `index.html`, no build step.
